## What was excluded

The music report includes queries such as `musical instrument crossword clue` (320/KD 12) and `thumbnails in a music collection crossword` (170/KD 7). These can seek one clue answer rather than a complete music puzzle. They do not justify creating artist or category pages for this site. Named publishers, worksheet unit/activity answer keys, and `music in the baroque period crossword puzzle answers` (50/KD 7) were also excluded as targets. Similar Thanksgiving answer-key searches can refer to somebody else's worksheet; this site supplies answers only for its own actual games.

There is no new SERP export in this pair. Earlier music and Christmas SERP evidence showed mixed entertainment, shopping and teaching pages; it does not establish every current result's intent. Query volume does not establish the searchers' ages, low KD does not guarantee ranking, and proper-name/era-specific demand has not been established by these files. The voice, city, instrument, and recording subthemes are editorial variety inside the music collection, not separately proven SEO markets.

## Allocation and practical value

The 100 new puzzles comprise 10 Thanksgiving, 60 music, 15 Christmas and 15 winter subjects. Thanksgiving gets a useful new collection with 16 actual games, including six existing puzzles. Music grows from 20 to 80 and gains a guide to different interests. Christmas grows from 12 to 27. Winter groups 15 new and five existing puzzles in one usable collection.

The Christmas and winter allocations use the earlier Christmas `480/KD 20` and winter `170/KD 11` root estimates recorded in [the November 12 research](expansion-200-research.md), together with the Christmas SERP export reviewed in [the 400-puzzle research](expansion-400-research.md). These are root-level signals; no separate demand was measured for each craft, dish or winter sport. The report's unlabelled normalized trend arrays are not interpreted as specific calendar months. The November 14 production date provides an editorial seasonal context independently of those arrays.

The added user value is distinct playable content, theme guidance, short direct clues, optional explanations, readable printing and separate answers. It is not the number of words of prose added to a page. Seven drafts too close to existing subjects were replaced after the full-catalog comparison. Required defining answers must survive layout selection. No duplicate keyword-variant pages are created, no audience is assumed to share one musical taste, and no medical or cognitive-benefit claim is made.
