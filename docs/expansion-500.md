# Expansion to 500 puzzles — November 14, 2026

This release adds 100 independent themed mini crosswords, bringing the collection to 500 in 100 series. The user explicitly requested useful expansion rather than arbitrary volume. The new subjects therefore concentrate on Thanksgiving (10), music (60), Christmas (15), and winter (15), using the supplied query evidence and a comparison with the published 400. These are content subjects, not 100 independently validated keyword opportunities.

All 400 previously published puzzle objects, boards, IDs, revisions, progress keys, first 80 manifest configurations, and the 100 original archived layouts are retained. New subjects are first editions with separate stable IDs. Source files, fixed seeds and required defining answers support reproducible generation.

## Selection and review

See [the research decision](expansion-500-research.md) and [the complete new-puzzle inventory](research/expansion-500-inventory.csv). The two new Semrush reports contain 874 Thanksgiving rows and 1,906 music rows; keyword variants are not added together as unique audiences. Individual subthemes without their own query evidence are editorial choices within a supported collection.

Seven near-duplicate draft subjects were replaced before release: general gift wrapping, addressed greeting cards, miniature village, animal tracks, general weather forecast, ice hockey, and accordion. Their replacements cover a folded star, pop-up card structure, Christmas pyramid, winter deer, wind chill, bobsled, and melodica. Familiar vocabulary still recurs when appropriate; changing clue wording alone was not accepted as proof of a new subject. The published 400 were not rewritten.

Source review covers ingredient/tool distinctions, sweet potatoes versus yams, recipe measurements, winter precipitation and wind-chill meanings, antlers versus horns, reed versus string mechanisms, and recording roles. Music-person clues use names, factual associations and song titles, without quoted lyrics or release-year trivia. Artificially truncated words were removed from the drafts; ordinary title spellings with an omitted final g are identified by the clues. Difficulty has not been measured with target readers, and some proper names or musical terms will be less familiar.

## Useful discovery and printing

- Thanksgiving now has one focused collection containing 16 real games, including six previously published puzzles. The page combines play, print and answer access rather than splitting keyword variants into separate pages.
- Music expands to 80 games with a guide to voices/groups, instruments, cities/styles, reading/making music, and records/gatherings.
- Christmas expands to 27 games; winter has 20 in a separate collection for comforts, nature, weather and pastimes.
- Shared selectors keep game-to-topic backlinks consistent. All 516 canonical pages remain within two ordinary HTML-link clicks from home, without relying on JavaScript or print copies.
- 1,617 HTML files include 500 playable games, 1,000 noindex print/answer copies, and 100 noindex original layouts. Each current game retains an edition-specific SVG card preview. There are no additional copies targeting free/PDF/with-answers/adults/seniors variants.
- Default printing retains 18pt clues and a larger grid on two US Letter pages; the compact option remains 13pt on one page. PDF saving is through the browser print dialog.

Validation results are recorded in [validation.md](validation.md). New-game keyboard completion, saved-progress restoration, mobile containment and both print modes are checked separately from clue review. Existing 400 objects are compared directly with the pre-expansion baseline. Actual indexing, reader difficulty and physical-printer output remain separate from local checks.
