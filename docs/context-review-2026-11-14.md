# Context and name-recall review — November 14, 2026

The user prioritizes useful easy, themed, large-print and printable puzzles over adding pages. This pass completes the remaining 66 decisions from the initial 500-game triage: 52 grids were replaced and 14 were retained with 18 simpler clues. Together with the preceding 27 specialist-priority replacements, 79 grids and 14 clue-only games address all 93 initially flagged targets. The collection remains 500 games with the same permanent URLs.

## Actual changes

The replacements remove specialized weaving, plant-anatomy, nautical-chart and music-theory requirements, plus performer birthplace, record-label, guitar-name and detailed lineup recall. Their new subjects use basic sewing, a rubber stamp, compost, familiar birds and garden scenes, a community show, everyday holiday food and ordinary old-song title words. This is a change to the selected answers and grids, with matching introductions, tips and three actual-answer notes, rather than more prose around the old answers. The 52 new boards have 582 clues, 10–12 each, within 11 × 11.

The 14 retained games have stable answers, placements and revision keys. Their 18 edits give direct definitions, a familiar name pairing, component-word hints or a first-letter clue. They remain labeled interest subjects. A reader unfamiliar with flowers, state capitals or a particular performer may still find them harder. The [18-clue change log](research/context-clue-changes-2026-11-14.json) preserves before/after wording.

These counts describe the inventory, not quality or ranking thresholds.
