The 100 new puzzles have 1,116 clues, 100 different introductions and 300 answer explanations. Every new board has 10–12 clues, is connected and fits within 11 × 11. Each source requires defining answers to survive actual board selection. Words range from familiar household objects to directly defined specialist terms; difficulty has not been measured with target readers.

The current collection contains 400 games in 80 series, 4,476 distinct answer/clue pairs, 2,202 distinct answers, 400 different introductions and 1,200 answer explanations. No exact answer/clue pair or introduction repeats. Eight new clues initially matching older clues were revised in the new sources only. Within a series, each puzzle differs from every other by at least three answers each way, with answer-set Jaccard no greater than 0.60. These are internal review signals rather than a Google quality threshold.

The source review paid particular attention to flower heads versus individual florets, pine seed cones versus pollen cones, bracts versus petals, fungi versus plants, spiders versus insects, and hominy versus ordinary ground corn. Mechanical, marine, craft and theater definitions distinguish components from their functions. Multiword grid entries and abbreviated forms are identified in their clues. No copyrighted dialogue, current celebrity trivia or medical-benefit claim is required.

## Research and discovery

See `expansion-400-research.md` for the eight supplied keyword rows and three SERP exports. A focused Christmas collection is a future seasonal candidate, while this release retains the five existing curated topic pages and the clean canonical main filters.

The sitemap has 413 canonical URLs, including all 400 playable games. There are 1,314 HTML files, 800 noindex print copies, 100 noindex previous editions and 400 edition-specific SVG card previews. All indexable pages remain discoverable within two ordinary HTML-link clicks from the home page. No individual puzzle was duplicated for a keyword variant.

## Validation scope

Production build, type checks, structural/content validation, generation reproducibility, browser regression checks, and operational checks for the new games are recorded in `validation.md` after completion. The previous 300 objects are compared directly with their pre-expansion baseline.

Each new game has a clue print route and a separate answer route. Local PDF checks confirm one US Letter page with all expected text and no grid/clue/footer overlap. The existing 13 pt clue size is unchanged; pagination checks do not certify an 18 pt standard or physical printer legibility. Mobile checks use a 390-pixel emulated viewport. Reader testing, actual indexing and live field performance remain separate from these checks.
