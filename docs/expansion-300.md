# Expansion to 300 puzzles — November 13, 2026

The user's target is 300 independent puzzles. This batch adds 80 first editions in sixteen five-puzzle series to the existing 220. It preserves all previous current puzzle objects, their answer placements, revisions and progress keys, and all 100 archived original layouts. Advertising changes are outside this batch.

## Scope and editorial choices

The 80 new puzzles contain 893 clue entries, 80 different introductions, 80 subject notes, 80 solving tips and 240 explanations tied to answers that actually appear in the finished grids. Every new board has 10–12 entries and fits within 11 × 11. Defining words are required in both source selection and validation, including GOLDENGATE, CARAPACE, DAUBER and FULCRUM. Short familiar answers support the longer names.

The current total is 300 puzzles in 60 series, 3,360 distinct answer/clue pairs, 1,859 distinct answers, 300 distinct introductions and 900 answer explanations. No complete clue sets, exact answer/clue pairs, or introductions are repeated. Familiar answers recur in different contexts; the audit separately reports answer overlap. Within each series, any pair has at least three different answers in each direction and answer-set Jaccard similarity no greater than 0.60. These are internal editorial signals, not Google quality thresholds.

The existing Semrush research informed the site's large-print/printable direction. This batch is editorial expansion within that direction, not evidence that every new title has measured search demand or a measured older audience. No new keyword volume or age-specific demand is claimed. The nature and science puzzles have direct definitions, but specialist terms can still be unfamiliar; reader difficulty feedback remains useful.

The content review distinguished a chrysalis from a cocoon, lunar phases from eclipses, a magnetic compass reference from geographic north, corms from bulbs and tubers, and a lever's force advantage from energy creation. Landmark clues specify the location and object rather than conflating cities, states and monuments. United States liquid-measure clues identify the measurement system. No contemporary sports trivia, medical claims or current visiting arrangements are required.

## Delivery and checks

Each puzzle has one playable URL, one noindex print route and one noindex answer route. The existing five curated interest collections remain; no new thin keyword landing pages were created. Main-collection theme and text filters discover the new subjects. The sitemap contains 313 canonical URLs, including all 300 games. The build contains 1,014 HTML files, including 600 print copies and 100 previous-edition pages.

Detailed command outcomes and local artifact paths are recorded in `validation.md` after verification. The local print audit checks one-page US Letter output, complete text and layout, with the existing 13 pt clue size; it does not establish physical printer legibility or an 18 pt print standard. Browser emulation and automated accessibility checks do not replace testing with target readers.
