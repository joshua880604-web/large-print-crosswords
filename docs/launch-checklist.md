# First release

## Prepared

- Astro static site, local system fonts and no third-party runtime requests.
- 400 editorial mini crosswords across 80 series; 4,476 clues and 1200 answer explanations.
- 400 playable pages and 800 printable puzzle/answer pages, plus six interest collections.
- Local progress saving and optional sound, large clues, and enlarged board.
- Default 18pt two-page printing, with a compact 13pt single-page choice; separate puzzle/answer routes.
- Christmas collection groups twelve existing puzzles with direct play/print and return links.
- Unique indexable titles/descriptions, production canonical URLs, sitemap and robots file.
- WebSite/WebPage and matching visible breadcrumb data; local sharing image.
- Post-build SEO audit across all 1315 HTML pages.
- The earlier 220-puzzle editorial reading is recorded in [the November 13 review](puzzle-review-2026-11-13.md); subsequent source reviews and validation are recorded with the [300](expansion-300.md) and [400](expansion-400.md) expansions. Earlier answer placements and progress keys are retained.
- Print copies excluded from indexing to avoid competing utility pages.

## Before public launch

- Review clues and actual difficulty with US target users. Verify touch input on real iOS/Android keyboards and screen-reader use; desktop emulation does not replace this.
- Cloudflare Pages and the custom domain have been configured by the user. Verify actual DNS, HTTPS, response status and redirects on the live domain once outbound access is available.
- Hosting/privacy details identify Cloudflare. Add an actual monitored contact address if needed; none has been invented.
- Test a physical US Letter printout at default scale. Printed size and pagination can vary by browser/printer.
- User has verified Search Console ownership; submit the sitemap using docs/search-console.md and inspect representative URLs.

## Content strategy

The supplied Semrush exports support large-print and printable intent. The November 12 bulk and sports/music SERP exports also inform theme selection; they do not establish the searchers' ages or revenue. See [the expansion research](expansion-200-research.md). The home page covers the brand and large-print collection; `/printable/` explains printing and answers; puzzle pages provide actual distinct playable content. Do not create separate copies of a puzzle for `free`, `with answers`, `PDF`, `adults`, or `seniors`. The current target of 400 independent puzzles is complete, including the earlier geography/history/Texas pilot and two subsequent content expansions. See [the 400-puzzle expansion](expansion-400.md) and [the November 14 research](expansion-400-research.md). See [the 300-puzzle expansion](expansion-300.md) for the latest scope and checks. See [the pilot notes](place-history-pilot.md) for the limited evidence and scope. Filter query URLs retain their collection canonical rather than creating additional indexable landing pages.
