# First release

## Prepared

- Astro static site, local system fonts and no third-party runtime requests.
- 100 original themed mini crosswords, 20 subjects, 1,259 clues.
- 100 playable pages and 200 printable puzzle/answer pages.
- Local progress saving and optional sound, large clues, and enlarged board.
- Titles, descriptions, canonical URLs, sitemap and robots file.
- Print copies excluded from indexing to avoid competing utility pages.

## Before public launch

- Review clues and actual difficulty with US target users. Verify touch input on real iOS/Android keyboards and screen-reader use; desktop emulation does not replace this.
- Choose a hosting account, deploy `dist/`, connect DNS, and check the real domain.
- Update the hosting/privacy details and add an actual monitored contact address; none has been invented.
- Test a physical US Letter printout at default scale. Printed size and pagination can vary by browser/printer.
- Submit sitemap in Search Console after the domain is live.

## Content strategy

The supplied Semrush exports support large-print and printable intent. The home page covers the brand and large-print collection; `/printable/` explains printing and answers; puzzle pages provide actual distinct playable content. Do not create separate copies of a puzzle for `free`, `with answers`, `PDF`, `adults`, or `seniors`. Theme search demand and revenue estimates remain unverified. No 100-keyword SEO mapping was commissioned: the user clarified the target as 100 puzzles.
