# First release

## Prepared

- Astro static site, local system fonts and no third-party runtime requests.
- 500 editorial mini crosswords across 100 series; 5,590 clues and 1500 answer explanations.
- 500 playable pages and 1000 printable puzzle/answer pages, plus eight interest collections.
- Local progress saving and optional sound, large clues, and enlarged board.
- Default 18pt two-page printing, with a compact 13pt single-page choice; separate puzzle/answer routes.
- Christmas collection groups 27 puzzles with direct play/print and return links.
- Unique indexable titles/descriptions, production canonical URLs, sitemap and robots file.
- WebSite/WebPage and matching visible breadcrumb data; local sharing image.
- Post-build SEO audit across all 1696 HTML pages.
- The earlier 220-puzzle editorial reading is recorded in [the November 13 review](puzzle-review-2026-11-13.md); subsequent source reviews and validation are recorded with the [300](expansion-300.md) and [400](expansion-400.md) expansions. Earlier answer placements and progress keys are retained.
- Print copies excluded from indexing to avoid competing utility pages.

## Before public launch

- Review clues and actual difficulty with US target users. Verify touch input on real iOS/Android keyboards and screen-reader use; desktop emulation does not replace this.
- Cloudflare Pages and the custom domain have been configured by the user. Verify actual DNS, HTTPS, response status and redirects on the live domain once outbound access is available.
- Hosting/privacy details identify Cloudflare. Add an actual monitored contact address if needed; none has been invented.
- Test a physical US Letter printout at default scale. Printed size and pagination can vary by browser/printer.
- User screenshots on November 14 confirm the sitemap fetch succeeded with 414 discovered URLs before this expansion and three representative live tests were eligible for indexing. This build has 516 sitemap URLs; an actual indexed-page count is still unavailable. Keep the same sitemap address. See docs/search-console.md.

## Content strategy

The supplied Semrush exports support large-print and printable intent. The November 12 bulk and sports/music SERP exports also inform theme selection; they do not establish the searchers' ages or revenue. See [the expansion research](expansion-200-research.md). The home page covers the brand and large-print collection; `/printable/` explains printing and answers; puzzle pages provide actual distinct playable content. Do not create separate copies of a puzzle for `free`, `with answers`, `PDF`, `adults`, or `seniors`. The current target of 500 independent puzzles is complete. The latest 100 focus on Thanksgiving, music, Christmas and winter; see [the 500-puzzle scope](expansion-500.md) and [the research decisions](expansion-500-research.md). See [the 400-puzzle expansion](expansion-400.md) and [the November 14 research](expansion-400-research.md). See [validation.md](validation.md) for the release checks. See [the pilot notes](place-history-pilot.md) for the limited evidence and scope. Filter query URLs retain their collection canonical rather than creating additional indexable landing pages.
