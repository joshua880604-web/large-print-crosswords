# Audience and content priorities

The site serves US older adults and anyone who prefers an easy, readable crossword. The user's priorities are **easy, large print, printable, and genuinely themed**, particularly gardening, everyday nostalgia, small-town life, familiar old songs, everyday wellbeing, and basic Bible scenes. Content quality and reader usefulness take priority over puzzle counts. Do not expand the catalog merely to reach a round number.

## Authoring and review

- Use familiar everyday answers and short, direct American English clues. A direct definition does not make an obscure botanical, mechanical, or musical term easy. Do not introduce specialist words just to obtain a new answer set or a working grid.
- Prefer one clear idea per clue. Avoid convoluted exclusions, obscure dates, unfamiliar celebrity trivia, internet slang, memes, and current pop-culture references. Familiar song names can be used in context, but nostalgia is not a license for a difficult discography quiz.
- Preserve factual accuracy while simplifying. Check singular/plural, part of speech, plausible alternate answers, and whether the clue actually describes the selected answer. Do not replace an accurate definition with an inaccurate easier-sounding one.
- Give every puzzle a coherent subject and useful answer selection. Rewording an old clue, changing a title, or padding introductory prose does not create new reader value. Review the existing catalog before authoring.
- Treat health as ordinary daily-life vocabulary, without diagnosis, treatment, or memory-benefit claims. Keep Bible content optional and clearly identified, using basic stories and common names rather than denominational disputes or obscure verse-reference quizzes.
- Do not call model-generated content handmade or professionally edited. Board validation, duplicate checks, and automated completion are separate from factual, language, and target-reader review. Do not certify difficulty using a word-length cutoff or a keyword-frequency score.

## Product and discovery

- Preserve readable defaults, optional hints, sound off, no timer/account requirement, and separate answers. Printing defaults to 18pt on two US Letter pages, with a compact 13pt one-page alternative.
- Build topic pages around real useful puzzles and distinct reader intent. Do not create near-identical `easy`, `free`, `printable`, `PDF`, `large print`, or `seniors` copies. These phrases describe needs to fulfill, not guaranteed ranking weights.
- Keep permanent playable URLs and saved-progress keys stable. Wording-only edits can preserve a board. Answer or layout replacements require proper edition handling so saved letters remain associated with their original grid.
- Consult `docs/audience-content-standard.md` and the audience review ledger before the next expansion. Flagged existing puzzles require review; absence of a flag is not certification that a puzzle is easy.
